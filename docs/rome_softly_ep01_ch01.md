## Place lock, about 509 BC

Use this on IMG 03, IMG 04, IMG 07, IMG 08, and any other plate set in
the living city. Do not use it on IMG 02, IMG 05, or IMG 06.

> Rome on the left bank of the Tiber about 509 BC, a living Latin city at the ford by Tiber Island. Yellow-brown silty river, reed beds, one timber bridge with no iron. Hills with thatch, gardens, and fields between them: Palatine, Capitoline, Quirinal, Viminal, Esquiline, Caelian, Aventine. The Forum valley is gravel and beaten earth, not stone paving. The drain is an open channel. Earth ramparts, timber gates, a few stretches of grey-brown cappellaccio tuff. Houses are thatched huts and courtyard houses of mud-brick and timber with terracotta tiles. Temples are Etruscan: a high tuff podium, frontal timber stairs, a deep porch, widely spaced smooth columns, a broad timber roof with painted terracotta plaques in red, black, cream, and ochre. No marble, no concrete vault, no Colosseum, no aqueduct, no triumphal arch, no basalt road, no glass.

Plants: holm oak, oak, laurel, myrtle, fig, olive, vine, willow, poplar,
reeds, stone pine, cypress, wheat and spelt, sheep, goats, pigs. Morning
mist on the river is allowed. Do not write “haze” as a style word.

## Never, in this chapter's archaic plates

Marble. The Colosseum, the Pantheon, circus grandstands, triumphal
arches, aqueducts, concrete vaults, basalt paving, Corinthian or Ionic
marble colonnades. Segmented armour, rectangular shields, pila, red
uniforms, eagle standards, banners. Coins, gladiators, imperial toga
folds, clean-shaven archaic men, glass windows, stirrups, horseshoes,
crosses, bound books, readable writing.

the sources doubt them. The still does not illustrate the doubt.
